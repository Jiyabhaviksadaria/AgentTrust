# AgentTrust

> A Unified Explainable Framework for Evaluating the Safety, Trustworthiness, and Robustness of AI Agents

## 📖 Overview

AgentTrust is a research-driven framework designed to evaluate the overall trustworthiness of AI agents before deployment. As AI agents become increasingly autonomous across domains such as healthcare, finance, education, customer service, and software engineering, there is a growing need for standardized methods to assess whether these systems are safe, reliable, and trustworthy.

The framework evaluates AI agents using multiple dimensions, including safety, robustness, reliability, hallucination detection, explainability, consistency, privacy, and adversarial resilience, ultimately generating an interpretable Trust Score and deployment readiness report.

---

## 🎯 Research Motivation

Current AI evaluation methods primarily focus on isolated metrics such as accuracy, latency, or hallucination. However, no comprehensive framework exists to evaluate the overall safety and trustworthiness of AI agents across different domains.

AgentTrust aims to bridge this gap by providing a unified, explainable, and extensible evaluation framework.

---

## 🚀 Objectives

- Develop a standardized AI agent evaluation framework.
- Measure the safety and trustworthiness of AI agents.
- Evaluate robustness against adversarial attacks.
- Assess hallucination and reasoning reliability.
- Generate explainable trust reports.
- Compare AI agents across multiple application domains.
- Provide deployment readiness recommendations.
